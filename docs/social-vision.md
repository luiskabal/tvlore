# Social Vision

TVLore should not initially become a traditional social network.

The product does not initially need:

- Follower counts.
- Public feeds.
- Influencer mechanics.
- Algorithmic timelines.
- Comments.
- Direct messages.
- Social posting.
- Engagement farming.

The social philosophy should revolve around:

> Discovering common ground through entertainment.

## Future Social Capabilities

Future social interactions may include:

- Compare profiles.
- Taste compatibility.
- Common titles.
- Differences.
- Recommendations based on one person's history.
- Titles neither person has watched.
- Shared watchlists.
- Optional friend relationships.

These remain future capabilities unless explicitly promoted into a later milestone.

## Why This Matters Architecturally

Viewing history may become social data. That means privacy, authorization, and derived data boundaries matter from the start.

The backend must own:

- Profile comparison.
- Privacy checks.
- Compatibility calculations.
- Visibility rules.
- Match token resolution.
- Derived result generation.

The mobile app may display social results. It must not calculate what another user is allowed to see.

## Product Tone

TVLore should feel personal before it feels public.

The first social experience should help two people understand:

- What they share.
- Where they differ.
- What one could recommend to the other.
- What they might watch together.

This is a better early fit than feeds, likes, and engagement loops.
