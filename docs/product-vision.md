# Product Vision

TVLore is a mobile-first entertainment tracking application centered around personal viewing history.

The product begins as a tracker for:

- TV shows
- Seasons
- Episodes
- Movies

The long-term idea is broader than logging content. TVLore should progressively build a user's audiovisual taste profile from what they watch, finish, rate, revisit, abandon, or add to their library.

Possible brand direction:

> Track what you watch. Discover what you share.

This is not final branding.

## Product Philosophy

Tracking is the foundation, but the long-term value comes from what can be derived from tracking data.

TVLore should eventually help answer:

- What have I watched?
- What episode am I on?
- What should I continue watching?
- What movies and shows do I like most?
- What genres dominate my viewing history?
- What have my friends watched?
- What do another person and I have in common?
- Where do our tastes differ?
- What have I watched that they have not?
- What have they watched that I have not?
- What could we watch together that neither of us has seen?

This makes viewing history first-party product data, not a disposable UI preference.

## Differentiation

A generic tracker stops at status and progress.

TVLore should start there, then grow toward:

- Taste profile construction.
- Common-ground discovery between people.
- Privacy-respecting comparison.
- Explainable compatibility and difference insights.
- Watch-together suggestions based on authorized histories.

The MVP should not implement all of this, but the architecture must avoid blocking it later.

## Product Boundary

TVLore should not initially become a traditional social network. It does not need public feeds, follower counts, comments, direct messages, influencer mechanics, or algorithmic timelines.

The social product thesis is smaller and stronger:

> Discover common ground through entertainment.
